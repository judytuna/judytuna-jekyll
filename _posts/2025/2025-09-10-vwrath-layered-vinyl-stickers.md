Stickers!

## Design stuff

Original design as a png:
![Varinwrath Red Design]({{ '/assets/images/2025/09/varinwrath-red.PNG' | relative_url }})

Don't have access to layers, so I used the "trace" function in Silhouette Studio. I started at the lowest sentitivity and took a "snapshot" every time it selected a part I wanted, and worked my way up until I had the layers separated by color.

I was trying to decide if the grey vinyl layer should have a red vinyl layer under it and eye holes, or if the grey vinyl layer should be directly on the black vinyl layer with red vinyl eyes on top. I think the latter is better.

This is my Silhouette Studio file:
[vwrath vinyl-sticker]({{ '/assets/images/2025/09/varinwrath-sticker-vinyl.studio3' | relative_url }})

## Silhouette and material settings
Siser Permanent Vinyl, "EasyPSV Starling"
- black
- grey
- red
- white

Silhouette cutting settings from the PDF
- Autoblade at 3
- Force: 8
- Speed: 7

The SiserNA PDF says to use 3, but some of my cuts weren't deep enough, so I made it 4, but now it penetrates the paper backing sometimes. sigh.
