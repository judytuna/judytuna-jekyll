## Mugs used

Dmsky Sublimation Ceramic Mug. 12pack. 11oz/33ml. Each is packed in its own box (and a thin plastic bag). They came stacked 3 wide, 2 deep, 2 high in an outbox and included sublimation paper and heat tape, which I didn't use.

The side of the box says use 380-390F heat and time 70s. I tried that and it wasn't long enough.

## Mug press heat settings

On my Craftexpress mug press:
- 383 F (the 2nd highest setting)
- 2m45s
